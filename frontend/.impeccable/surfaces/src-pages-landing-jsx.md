## Direction contract

THESIS: A live operations board for one shop's money: the page reads like a working control room for Murugan's day, not a marketing brochure; it refuses the fintech hero (headline, phone mockup, feature cards).
OWN-WORLD: Warm off-white paper ground, deep teal-ink text, pale sky panels, whole panels filled in lime (sales/proof), violet (family money), signal orange (asked/attention), sun yellow (expenses/taxman). Heavy black display headlines with tight tracking, bold uppercase mono labels, large mono figures. Big rounded panels with thin ink borders, a dark scrolling payment ticker, a numbered left rail.
STORY: The visitor sees Murugan's demo day as a live board, understands that Naanayam separates sales from family money and asks in Tamil when unsure, sees the three proofs and the evaluation (synthetic, from the run), and opens the live demo.
